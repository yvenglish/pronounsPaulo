The Creative Team

Ethan is a photographer from Canada.

He works with Sofia and Daniel during weddings and outdoor sessions.

Sofia is responsible for the lights and Daniel works with the cameras.

They are very calm and organized during photoshoots.

Many clients are nervous at the beginning, but Ethan always helps them feel comfortable.

One couple says:

“We are afraid of looking awkward in pictures.”

Ethan smiles and says:

“You look amazing. Just relax and trust us.”

During the session, Sofia stands beside the couple while Daniel stays behind the camera.

The team works together all day and the atmosphere is always natural and relaxed.

At the end of the photoshoot, the clients say:

“You guys are incredible.”
